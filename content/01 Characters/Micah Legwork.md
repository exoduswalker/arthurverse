Micah is a Hyur from Sharlayan.

## Origin

Micah studied astrology in the colony of Idyllshire until 5 years later into the Garleans' conquest for Eorzea where he fled to Ishgard to continue his study.

## Story

### Heavensward

The Warrior of Light and his companions met Micah in the Athenaeum Astrologicum whilst exploring the Holy See of Ishgard for the first time.

Micah accompanied Arthur and Woolsey for the majority of the arc.
