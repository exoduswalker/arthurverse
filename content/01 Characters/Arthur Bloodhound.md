Arthur is the Warrior of Light introduced at the beginning of A Realm Reborn.

## Origin

Born Ala Mhigan, he fled for Little Ala Mhigo when he was young and met his adventuring partner Woolsey, who also hailed from Ala Mhigo. As the days grew by, he and Woolsey grew tired of living a downtrodden life in the hollow formation. There, he saw a chocobo carriage bound for Ul'dah, thus beginning the events of A Realm Reborn.

## Story

### A Realm Reborn

_The story is largely unchanged here other than Woolsey accompanies Arthur for the majority of the expansion._

### Heavensward

After the events in Ul'dah, he had to find something to camouflage himself in the Coerthan snow, and to conceal his identity as the so-called "assassin with royal blood on his hands" should he ever need to step out of Ishgard's frostbitten sanctuary.

During his first trip in Foundation with Alphinaud and Woolsey, he met [[Micah Legwork|Micah]], whom reveals himself to be a Sharlayan astrologian who moved to Ishgard after leaving Idyllshire 5 years after Garlemald occupied Ala Mhigo.

### Stormblood

Arthur and Woolsey looked forward to liberating their homeland the most after receiving word from Yda around the events of _Baelsar's Wall_.

During his time in the Far East, he met [[Kagerou Seisho|Kagerou]], who accompanied Arthur.
