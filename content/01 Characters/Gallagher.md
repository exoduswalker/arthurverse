Gallagher is a Hume from The Rak'tika Greatwood. He is the shard of [[Woolsey Bloodhound|Woolsey]] from the First, and is a priest of the Night's Blessed, often tasked with slaying Sin Eaters.

## Story

### Shadowbringers

The Scions and Emet-Selch meet Gallagher when they arrived in The Rak'tika Greatwood, almost engaging in combat until Y'shtola, under the name of "Master Matoya", ordered the Blessed to lower their weapons.

At the end of Bearing with It, Gallagher bids Runar farewell to accompany the Scions until later.

> [!quote] Gallagher
> If their work isn't done, then I'm afraid I must join Master Matoya and her friends to where they are bound for.
>
> To get rid of one problem, one must cut it at the roots. If they are bound for Eulmore, then I must see what lies ahead.
