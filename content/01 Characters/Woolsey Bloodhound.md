Born to a leader of an Ala Mhigan mercenary group called the "Bloodhounds", Woolsey found himself leading a small army of men at such a young age after having his life stolen by the Garlean Empire. He is [[Arthur Bloodhound|Arthur]]'s adventuring companion starting from A Realm Reborn.

## Origin

Woolsey was at the age of 18 when Garlemald came for Ala Mhigo. His scars were a result of fighting through imperial soldiers as he saw his father, Alexander, be murdered at the hands of Garleans in front of his eyes.

Woolsey ran towards Baelsar's Wall as fast as he could, causing him to be injured multiple times from the imperial soldiers' attacks and leaving him permanently blind on his left eye.

Since then, he has taken up the mantle of the Sanguine Wolf, a title reserved for the leader of the mercenary group, the Bloodhounds.

### Stormblood

Woolsey continued to accompany Arthur throughout the events of Stormblood, but would choose to stay in Ala Mhigo every time Doma and Kugane needed the attention of the Warrior of Light. During these moments, he accompanied the Ala Mhigan Resistance, calling upon his men whenever needed.

After the events of [The Resonant](https://ffxiv.consolegameswiki.com/wiki/The_Resonant), Woolsey confronted Fordola while she was in the custody of the Alliance, reminding her that she was born as and will always be an Ala Mhigan, no matter her circumstances as a child born in the Empire's occupation. Woolsey then left her to help M'naago to hold the residential district.

Near the end of the arc, Woolsey asked Arthur if he could accompany him to the Crystal Tower once he heard news of Arthur regaining consciousness, to which Arthur agreed.
